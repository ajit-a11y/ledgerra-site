What Ledgerra checks

Billing against your contracts

Prices, annual increases, discounts, minimum commitments and payment terms in each contract, compared with every Stripe subscription and invoice.

Stripe against your books

Every Stripe payout, fee, refund and dispute, matched to what was recorded in QuickBooks Online.

Vendor bills In development

Duplicate bills, changed bank details and rates above what you agreed, caught before you pay.

How it works

  1. Connect

    Sign in with Google, then connect Stripe, QuickBooks Online and the contract files you pick in Google Drive.

  2. Check

    Ledgerra reads each contract and compares its terms with your Stripe billing and your QuickBooks records, every day.

  3. Review

    Each gap shows the clause, invoice or payout behind it, so your team can decide what to fix.

How Ledgerra handles your data

Read-only during the beta
Ledgerra reads from Stripe and QuickBooks Online and changes nothing in them.
Only the Drive files you pick
Ledgerra uses Google's file picker, so it can open only the files you choose. It can't see the rest of your Drive.
Used only to run your checks
We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
Encrypted, and yours to delete
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Disconnect any service at any time, and close your account to have your data deleted.
